## Where CareerAgent-AI Differs

CareerAgent-AI is not positioned as:
- just an ATS optimizer
- just an autofill extension
- just an auto-apply bot

It is positioned as an **agentic career workflow platform** with emphasis on:
- orchestration
- approval gates
- traceability
- explainability
- operational state
- modular architecture for deployment and iteration
